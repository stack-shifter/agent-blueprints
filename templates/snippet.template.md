## Rule Title

The rule itself, stated as instructions to an agent. Imperative voice. No preamble,
no rationale unless the rationale changes behavior at the margin.

- Specific enough to be checkable.
- Short enough to read in one glance.
- Include the negative case when it is the load-bearing half.
